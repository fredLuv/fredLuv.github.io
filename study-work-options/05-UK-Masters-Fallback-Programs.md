# UK Master's Fallback Programs (CS, FinTech & Computational Finance)

This document evaluates 1-to-1.5 year UK Master's degree programs in London and the UK as a strategic fallback/pivot option, detailing start dates, tuition, application deadlines, and **January/Spring intake options**.

---

## 🇬🇧 Why a UK Master's is an Outstanding Fallback

1. **Short 1-Year Sprint**: UK Master's degrees are **12 months long** (3 terms), saving 50% time compared to 2-year US/Canadian degrees.
2. **2-Year Graduate Route (PSW) Open Work Visa**: Graduating automatically grants a **2-Year Unsponsored Open Work Permit** in the UK, allowing you to work for any London hedge fund or tech firm with zero visa friction.
3. **Canary Wharf / London Recruiter Pipeline**: Top London funds actively recruit from UCL, Imperial, KCL, and QMUL.

---

## 🏛️ GROUP A: Top-Tier Target Programs (Fall / September Start)

### 1. UCL (University College London) – MSc Computational Finance
* **Location**: Bloomsbury & Canary Wharf, London
* **Start Date**: **September**
* **Duration**: **1 Year (12 Months)**
* **Tuition (International)**: **~£41,000 (~$52,000 USD)**
* **Application Window**: Opens **October 1**, closes **June 30** (Rolling admissions; early application recommended).
* **Strategic Value**: World #9 university; direct feeder to Canary Wharf buy-side trading desks.

### 2. Imperial College London – MSc Financial Technology (FinTech) / MSc Computing (AI)
* **Location**: South Kensington, London
* **Start Date**: **September**
* **Duration**: **1 Year (12 Months)**
* **Tuition (International)**: **~£42,200 (~$54,000 USD)**
* **Application Window**: Opens **October 1**, closes **May 31** (Rolling rounds).
* **Strategic Value**: World #2 university (QS 2025/2026); top-tier STEM & Quantitative reputation.

### 3. King's College London (KCL) – MSc Computational Finance / Data Science
* **Location**: Strand / Bush House, Central London
* **Start Date**: **September**
* **Duration**: **1 Year (12 Months)**
* **Tuition (International)**: **~£35,240 (~$45,000 USD)**
* **Application Window**: Opens **October 1**, closes **July 31**.
