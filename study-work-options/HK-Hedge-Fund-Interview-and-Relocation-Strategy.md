## 🇭🇰 Does the September Interview Strategy Apply to Hong Kong?

### **YES, 100%.** 

While Hong Kong does not have as extreme a 4-week August shutdown as Western Europe, **September remains the peak hiring sprint in HK for 3 major reasons**:

1. **Global Unified Hiring Cycles**: Top firms like **Citadel, Jane Street, Millennium, and Point72** run global hiring quotas across NYC, London, and HK concurrently. Engineering Managers and Bar Raisers in NYC/London coordinate HK interview loops.
2. **Q3/Q4 Budget Sprint**: September is the final window for HK Managing Directors to lock in headcount before Q4 year-end bonus allocations and budget freezes.
3. **The 4-Week August Prep Advantage**: Using August to master low-latency architecture, concurrency, and order book design ensures you enter HK loops at peak competitiveness.

## 🛂 Hong Kong Immigration & Visa Pathways

Hong Kong offers one of the fastest immigration systems for top-tier tech talent:

1. **GEP (General Employment Policy) Visa**:
   * Sponsored directly by the employer (Citadel HK, Jane Street, Millennium).
   * Fast processing: **2 to 4 weeks**.
2. **TTPS (Top Talent Pass Scheme / 高才通)**:
   * **Category A**: Annual income > HK$2.5M (~$320k USD) in the preceding year ➔ **2-Year Unsponsored Open Work Visa**.
   * **Category B**: Bachelor's degree from a Top 100 global university + 3 years of work experience ➔ **2-Year Unsponsored Open Work Visa**.
3. **QMAS (Quality Migrant Admission Scheme / 优才新政)**:
   * Ethan scores **8/12** under the 12-criteria assessment (Age, Degree, Language, 9 yrs MNC Tech/Fintech experience, Income > HK$1M).
