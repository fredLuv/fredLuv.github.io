## 🇬🇧 United Kingdom (London): The High-Earner & Quant Pathway

### Pros:
1. **Unrivaled Buy-Side Job Density**: Home to London's Mayfair and Canary Wharf financial districts (Citadel Europe, Millennium, Man Group, Point72, Brevan Howard, Marshall Wace).
2. **Superior Compensation**: Senior SDEs and Quant Devs earn **$250,000 – $450,000+ USD equivalent**, significantly outpaying Canadian tech roles.
3. **Global Talent Visa (3-Year PR)**: Fast-track to UK Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR) in 36 months without employer sponsorship.
4. **4-Year Foreign Income Exemption (FIG Regime)**: 100% tax exemption on foreign investment gains and foreign income for your first 4 years in the UK.

### Cons:
* Higher cost of living in Central London.

---

## 🇨🇦 Canada (Toronto/Waterloo): The Fast Permanent Residency Pathway

### Pros:
1. **Fastest & Easiest PR**: Graduating from a 1-year Waterloo CS Master's gives an automatic **3-Year Open Work Permit (PGWP)** and direct access to **Ontario Masters Stream PR** without needing a job offer.
2. **Proximity to the US**: Easy access to US markets and seamless travel between the US and Canada.
3. **Safety & Stability**: High quality of life, multicultural environment.

### Cons:
1. **Lower Tech & Quant Salaries**: Canadian software salaries peak around **CAD $200k–$250k**, creating a significant earning ceiling compared to London or NYC.
2. **High Combined Tax Rate**: Ontario’s top marginal tax rate hits **53.53%** above CAD $235k.
