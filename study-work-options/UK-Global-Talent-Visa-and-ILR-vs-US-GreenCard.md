# UK Permanent Residency (ILR) vs. US Green Card: Strategic Comparison

This document records the strategic analysis comparing **UK Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR / UK Permanent Residency)** via the **Global Talent Visa** versus the **US Green Card (EB-1 / EB-2 / Marriage)** for senior software engineers and quant developers.

## 📊 Summary Comparison: UK ILR vs. US Green Card

| Parameter | 🇬🇧 UK Global Talent Visa (GTV) ➔ ILR | 🇺🇸 US Green Card (EB-2 / EB-3 / EB-1) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Time to Permanent Residency** | ⚡ **3 Years** (Exceptional Talent) / 5 Yrs (Skilled Worker) | ⏳ **5 to 7+ Years** (Due to China-born EB backlog) |
| **Employer Sponsorship Needed?**| ❌ **No (100% Self-Petitioned & Unsponsored)** | Yes (for PERM/EB-2/3) or Strict Self-Petition (EB-1A/NIW) |
| **Job & Entrepreneurial Freedom**| **100% Free** (Switch hedge funds, contract, or start a company) | Bound to H-1B sponsor until I-485 EAD |
| **Time to Citizenship (Passport)**| **4 Years Total** (3 Yrs GTV + 1 Yr ILR) | **8 to 12+ Years Total** (Queue + 5 Yrs GC) |
| **Worldwide Tax Burden (IRS vs. HMRC)**| 🌍 **Residence-Based Tax** (Stop paying UK tax if you leave UK) | 🇺🇸 **Worldwide Tax** (IRS taxes global income for life) |
| **First 4 Years Tax Relief** | 🎁 **4-Year FIG Regime** (Foreign income/gains 100% exempt) | No foreign tax exemption for US tax residents |
| **Certainty & Rule Stability** | **Objective Statutory Rules** (Pass 3 yrs + Life in UK test = 99%+ ILR) | Subject to PERM audits, H-1B lotteries, visa delays |
| **Spouse Work Rights** | ✅ **Full Open Work Authorization** from Day 1 | Dependent on H-4 EAD (requires spouse I-140 approval) |

## 👑 UK Global Talent Visa (Digital Technology Stream)

### 1. Key Advantages
1. **Unsponsored Freedom**: You can work for Citadel Europe, Millennium, Man Group, Point72, switch firms instantly, or launch your own fintech company.
2. **Fast-Track ILR (3 Years)**: As a senior engineer with 9 years of experience (AWS, ExodusPoint, Goldman Sachs), you qualify under **Exceptional Talent**, unlocking UK Permanent Residency in 36 months.
3. **British Passport in Year 4**: Eligible for UK Citizenship 12 months after receiving ILR.

* **Mandatory Criterion (Leadership)**:
  * High salary proof (W-2s / pay slips at AWS & ExodusPoint).
  * Architecture ownership (Lead Architect for AWS AI Observability platform supporting 15+ GenAI models).

  * ExodusPoint: Real-time PnL/risk streaming infrastructure processing tens of billions in monthly volume, scaling Apache Druid memory maps for 100GB+ position snapshots (p99 query latency cut by 50%).
  * AWS: Multi-region Java log ingestion pipeline optimization (p99 API latency slashed by 87%).
* **3 Letters of Recommendation**:
  * Signed by C-level executives or Senior Engineering Directors/MDs at AWS, ExodusPoint, and Goldman Sachs.
