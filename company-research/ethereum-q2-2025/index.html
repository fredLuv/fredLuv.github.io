Strategic Significance of EIP-4844

  • EIP-4844 introduced **Blobs**, dedicated data storage spaces that allow L2 transactions to bypass L1 execution fees.
  • This caused user fees on L2s like Base and Arbitrum to fall by over 90%, fueling the 12.7x transaction volume expansion.
  • While L2s capture high operational margins (as shown in the waterfall), they still depend on Ethereum L1 for permanent, decentralized settlement and consensus security.

Rerate Thesis.

Ethereum's investment narrative is undergoing a fundamental business-model transition: from a congested monolithic execution chain to the ultimate secure global settlement layer.

01

Old Monolithic Narrative

  • Monolithic Scaling: Attempting to run all smart contracts and trades directly on L1 mainnet.
  • Google-like CAC: Skyrocketing gas fees ($50+ per trade) priced out retail users and restricted scalability.
  • Yield Model: Solely reliant on on-chain execution volumes to drive deflationary mechanics.
02

The Modular Paradigm

  • Layer-2 Rollups: Scale execution indefinitely while retaining Ethereum's underlying security guarantees.
  • Settlement Focus: L1 transitions into a high-margin premium ledger for state settlement and finality.
  • Security Moat: $89.25 Billion of staked capital secures the chain, generating institutional-grade yield.
03

Institutional Grade Capture

  • Spot ETF Approval: Nearly $4.0 Billion in Q2 inflows connects Ethereum directly to tradfi asset allocators.
  • RWA Dominance: Absolute dominance in tokenized treasuries, assets, and stablecoins.
  • Ultra-Sound Yield: Yield backed by protocol fees and issuance offers structured base-rate utility.

  • **Short-Term Pain:** Reduced mainnet fee burns resulting from modular scaling temporarily challenges the "deflationary" narrative.
