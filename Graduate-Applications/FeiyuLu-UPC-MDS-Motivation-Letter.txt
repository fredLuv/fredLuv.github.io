Motivation Letter
Feiyu Lu | UPC Master in Data Science

I am applying to UPC Barcelona School of Informatics' Master in Data Science because my career has been built around the production systems that make serious data science possible: reliable data ingestion, high-volume pipelines, low-latency analytics, observability, and systems that remain correct under operational pressure. After nine years as a software engineer across Amazon, Goldman Sachs, ExodusPoint Capital Management, and AWS, I want to deepen the mathematical, statistical, machine-learning, and data-management foundations behind the systems I already build at scale.

My interest in data science began during my junior year at Tufts University, where I earned a B.S. in Computer Science. As a Data Scientist Intern at DigitasLBi, I used Python and Spark with TF-IDF, Word2Vec, and K-Means to cluster more than 50,000 search phrases for campaign analysis. That project gave me an early view of how raw behavioral data becomes business insight when representation, algorithms, and scalable computation are designed together.

Since then, my work has moved from analytical prototypes to high-scale production systems. At Amazon Advertising, I designed Java microservices and event-driven AWS pipelines for billing and interactive video advertising. These services processed millions of telemetry and billing events per day and required careful engineering around concurrency, idempotency, retries, monitoring, and global reliability. The experience taught me that data science succeeds only when the underlying data platform is trustworthy.

At ExodusPoint Capital Management, I built real-time PnL and risk infrastructure for fixed income and equities using Java, Kafka, Apache Druid, WebSocket, and Protobuf. One major challenge involved optimizing concurrent Java services and in-memory Druid workflows that generated 100GB+ position snapshots per minute while reducing p99 query latency by 50% during market spikes. This work strengthened my interest in time-sensitive analytics, data modeling, optimization, and the statistical reasoning needed to support financial decision-making.

In my current role at Amazon Web Services, I work on distributed log-ingestion and observability infrastructure. I have optimized Kinesis provisioning and ingestion throttling, reduced authorization latency through caching, and supported AI observability workflows for production GenAI services. As AI systems become more widely deployed, the problems that interest me most are data quality, scalable processing, monitoring, governance, and responsible production operation.

UPC's Master in Data Science is compelling because it treats data science as the intersection of data management and data analytics. Courses such as Algorithms, Data Structures and Databases; Data Warehousing; Statistical Inference and Modelling; Big Data Management; Machine Learning; Mining Unstructured Data; Semantic Data Management; Cloud Computing and Big Data Analytics; MLOps; Information Retrieval and Recommender Systems; and Optimization Techniques for Data Mining map directly to the gaps I want to close. I also value the program's English instruction, international environment, and location in Barcelona, where I could combine graduate study with focused product exploration in AI and financial-data infrastructure.

After the program, my goal is to become a stronger senior technical leader and architect for data-intensive and AI-enabled systems, especially in cloud infrastructure and financial technology. I would bring UPC a production engineer's perspective: I know how systems behave when data volume is high, users depend on them, and failures have real consequences. I now want the academic depth to design those systems with stronger mathematical and data-science judgment.
