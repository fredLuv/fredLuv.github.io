Important correction: H1B does not have SEVIS. For change of status, the school issues a Form I-20 for F1 change of status, the student pays the I-901 SEVIS fee, then files Form I-539 with USCIS. CPT work can start only after F1 status is effective and the school has issued a CPT-authorized I-20 for the specific employer, site, dates, and full-time/part-time basis.

Trine University

SEVP school to verify in SEVP list COS timing risk Hybrid graduate programs

Trine is common in Day 1 CPT discussions because its international graduate pages explicitly discuss CPT eligibility, full-time CPT limits, employer/course matching, and hybrid graduate study. For H1B-to-F1 COS, the biggest issue is timing: Trine says students in H1B status cannot access the CPT application until the change of visa status is processed.

Relevant programsMSIS, MSBA, MSEM, MBA and related hybrid graduate options
Tuition math$575/credit; 30-credit degree tuition about $17,250 before fees/insurance/CPT course fees
Full-time loadMinimum 6 credits/semester for F1 graduate students per Trine CPT page
Seattle realityConfirm campus, in-person attendance cadence, and whether Seattle worksite is acceptable on CPT I-20
  • Trine says CPT is available only if the work matches the degree and the student is enrolled in the required CPT course.
  • If planning OPT after the degree, keep full-time CPT below 12 months; Trine advises 364 days max.
  • Apply early: Trine states CPT processing can take up to 14 business days and should be submitted 14-30 days before the employment start date.

Westcliff University

SEVP school to verify in SEVP list Professional tuition tier Graduate CPT handbook

Westcliff is another common Day 1 CPT path because its F1 handbook describes CPT as integral to programs with Practical Learning Experience requirements, including graduate College of Technology and Engineering programs. It is stronger for Ethan's CS/data narrative if choosing MSCS or MSIT rather than a generic business degree.

Relevant programsMSCS, MSIT, MSEM, MBA; confirm the exact Professional / CPT-eligible format
Tuition mathMaster's $835/credit or Professional $875/credit; 36-credit MSCS about $30,060-$31,500 before fees
CPT course costInternship/CPT course is 1 credit per 16-week semester or 0.5 credit per session
Seattle realityNearest practical campus may still require travel; confirm hybrid attendance and CPT worksite match
  • Westcliff says graduate COTE students must complete Practical Learning Experience hours before graduation.
  • The handbook says new students' earliest CPT start date can be the program start date on the I-20 if processed in SEVIS before that date.
  • For full-time CPT, keep the cumulative total under 12 months if preserving OPT matters.
