Curriculum Highlights

Master in Innovation and Research in Informatics (MIRI)

4 semesters, 120 ECTS, face-to-face, English.

Specializations: Advanced Computing; Computer Networks and Distributed Systems; High Performance Computing; Computer Graphics/VR. Relevant subjects include Concurrence, Parallelism and Distributed Systems; Statistical Modelling and Experimental Design; Network Architecture and Management; Algorithmic Methods for Mathematical Models.

Master in Data Science (MDS)

4 semesters, 120 ECTS, face-to-face, English; full-time workload listed as 20h lectures + 20h personal work per week.

Core: Algorithms, Data Structures and Databases; Data Warehousing; Multivariate Analysis; Process-Oriented Data Science; Statistical Inference and Modelling; Big Data Management; Machine Learning; Mining Unstructured Data; Semantic Data Management. Useful electives: Advanced Machine Learning, Cloud Computing and Big Data Analytics, MLOps, Information Retrieval and Recommender Systems, Optimization Techniques for Data Mining, Data Visualization.

Master in Artificial Intelligence (MAI)

3 semesters, 90 ECTS, full-time, face-to-face, English.

Compulsory: Computational Intelligence, Computational Vision, Introduction to Human Language Technology, Multiagent Systems, Machine Learning, Planning and Approximate Reasoning. Relevant electives: Big Data Analytics, Deep Learning, Reinforcement Learning, Probabilistic Graphical Models, Responsible AI, Intelligent Data Analysis Applications in Business.

Master's degree in Statistics and Operations Research (UPC-UB)

1.5 academic years, 90 ECTS, afternoons, face-to-face.

Relevant paths include Operations Research and Data Science. Courses include Continuous Optimisation, Optimisation in Data Science, Risk Quantification, Simulation, Linear and Generalised Linear Models, Econometric Analysis, Statistical Programming and Databases.
