Statement of Purpose

Feiyu (Ethan) Lu | Northwestern SPS Accelerated M.S. in Data Science

I am applying to Northwestern's Accelerated M.S. in Data Science because I want to turn nine years of high-scale production engineering experience into a stronger foundation for leading data engineering and AI infrastructure. My career has been built around systems where data is not an afterthought: it is the product surface, the operational signal, and often the source of risk. Northwestern's one-year, cohort-based format is especially important to me because it respects the opportunity cost of stepping away from senior engineering work while still offering the concentrated academic structure I need.

My interest in data began during my junior year at Tufts, when I worked as a data science intern at DigitasLBi. I used Python and Spark with TF-IDF, Word2Vec, and K-Means to cluster more than 50,000 search phrases for campaign analysis. Since then, my work has moved from applied analytics prototypes to production systems at much larger scale. At Amazon Advertising, I built AWS-based Java microservices and distributed event pipelines using DynamoDB, SQS/SNS, Lambda, and Fargate to process millions of ad events per day. At ExodusPoint, I built real-time PnL and risk infrastructure for fixed income and equities, using Java, Kafka, Apache Druid, WebSocket, and Protobuf to support sub-second analytics in trading workflows. One of the hardest technical challenges was optimizing in-memory Druid and Java/Spring Boot services that generated 100GB position snapshots every minute while reducing p99 latency by 50%.

At AWS, I now work on distributed log ingestion and observability infrastructure. I have optimized Kinesis provisioning and PutLogEvents throttling, reduced authorization latency with caching, and built tools that connect CloudWatch Logs, IAM, and Secrets Manager. These projects have shown me that the next generation of reliable AI systems will depend on excellent data engineering: governed data flows, scalable storage, model-aware observability, and interfaces that help humans trust automated decisions.

Northwestern's Data Engineering specialization fits this direction precisely. Database Systems, Practical Machine Learning, Data Engineering with Go, Foundations of Data Engineering, Data Governance, Ethics, and Law, Analytics Application Engineering, and the Capstone would let me connect my production background with a more systematic graduate-level framework. I am particularly drawn to the mix of on-campus cohort learning and online technical courses, because I learn best when theory is tested against real systems and peer discussion.

After the program, my goal is to become a senior technical leader designing reliable data and AI infrastructure for cloud and financial technology organizations. I want to build systems that are not only fast and scalable, but also interpretable, well-governed, and useful to decision-makers. Northwestern SPS offers the rare combination I am seeking: an accelerated path, a strong university brand, and a curriculum that aligns directly with the data engineering problems I have spent my career solving.
